About

↑ Top

I'm a third-year PhD student in mathematics at the Einstein Institute of Mathematics, Hebrew University of Jerusalem, advised by Ari Shnidman and Borys Kadets. I'm broadly interested in algebraic geometry and number theory; specifically, I work on algebraic cycles.

Before this I did my BSc in mathematics at Tel-Aviv University, and my MSc at the Hebrew University under Ari Shnidman, on a Cohen–Lenstra-type arithmetic statistics question about the distribution of points on an elliptic curve mod p, as p varies.
