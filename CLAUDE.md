## Footer "Latest update" date

The footer shows "Latest update: <date>" on the left of the footer row, opposite the name (design system Footer component). It is typed in by hand, not generated. Every time you change the site's content, layout or styling, update the date to today's date in both places:

- `index.html`
- `_layouts/writing.html`

Use the day, month, year format, for example "30 September 2026". Do not update it for the automated daily arXiv data fetch.
